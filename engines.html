The backend tech and frameworks driving our custom gaming projects.

Geared 2 Engine

Status: In Active Planning • Framework: Heavily Modded TurboWarp • Built For: L2D Project

Our upcoming powerhouse framework. Running on a heavily modded TurboWarp platform layer, Geared 2 introduces a completely overhauled custom physics system designed from scratch. It is engineered specifically to handle dense zombie hordes, custom bullet trajectories, and fast survival-shooter mechanics without sacrificing web runtime performance.

TurboWarp (Geared + Mod)

Status: Operational • Framework: Modded TurboWarp • Built For: ORIGINS 3 & 4

An upgraded variant of our horror setup. The Geared + Mod tier introduces enhanced graphical adjustments, memory layout optimizations, and advanced scripting logic extensions to match the decaying, atmospheric requirements of the later franchise installments.

TurboWarp (Geared Mod)

Status: Live & Stable • Framework: Modded TurboWarp • Built For: ORIGINS 1 & 2

Our primary runtime framework. Built on top of a heavily optimized custom TurboWarp compiler layer, the standard Geared Mod allows for rapid scene transitions, high-fidelity sound cue processing, and strict jumpscare tracking variables required for atmospheric horror layouts.
